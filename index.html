Go / no-go rules

Nayla runs without a crew, so nobody can bring food or pick her up mid-course. Medical is only at Start, WS6 Jemplang, and Finish. The stretch from WS6 to Finish is 25.5 km with no medical point. Thresholds are proposals to confirm with her coach and the event medical team.

Legs

  • Quad pain 7/10 or higher: walk the next descent and cut pace by 10%.
  • Sharp or one-sided pain, or limping: stop at the next water station and ask for medical.

Stomach and fuel

  • Nausea once: switch to water and electrolyte for 30 minutes, then resume gel.
  • Vomiting, or nausea twice in a row: no gel until it settles; tell medical at the next point.
  • Diarrhea or GI episode: tap "GI episode" so it is logged. Two or more in one segment: pause gel, use water and electrolyte only, and tell medical at the next point. (Proposal, confirm with coach and medical.)
  • Cannot keep any fluid down for 60 minutes: stop and seek medical help.

Heat and cold

  • Dizziness, confusion, or cramps with heat signs: stop in shade, cool down, and get medical now.
  • Shivering at night: add a layer and eat; if shivering continues or speech slurs, get medical now.

Mind

  • Wanting to quit: run the 10-minute rule first (eat, drink, walk 5 minutes), then decide.
  • Behind plan by more than 30 minutes at any checkpoint: re-pace, cut stops to the minimum, and do not chase with surges.

Clock and stop budget

  • Projected finish after 18:45 WIB: stop and decide whether to continue. Do not push through a cut-off risk.
  • Planned stops are 57 min. If moving pace matches BTR 2026, a 15 h finish allows about 2 h 14 min of total stops. Log every unplanned stop.
